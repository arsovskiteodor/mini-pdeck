# 2026-10-27

## Session 1: Kickoff and MCU direction
**Phase/step:** 1 Decide, 01 Lock the spec

### Done
- Defined the project: mini macropad with about 4 switches and 2 rotary encoders, as a learning
  project to design, order and test a real PCB cheaply.
- Recommended an MCU *module* over a bare chip for v1. A bare RP2040 needs flash, a crystal, a
  regulator and USB circuitry, and comes in QFN (needs assembly). A module removes those unknowns.
  Plan: v2 can be a bare RP2040.
- Initial pick: Seeed XIAO RP2040 (11 GPIO). Revisited on 2026-10-28 once the OLED was added.
- Created the 13-step checklist (4 phases) as a web page. This repo mirrors it in `STATUS.md`.

### Found / learned
- JLCPCB 2-layer boards are a fixed low price up to 100 × 100 mm; above that they get expensive.
  Shipping usually costs more than the boards.
  Source: https://hwdocs.hackclub.dev/user-contrib-guides/jlc-cost-optimizing/
- KiCad 10 is the current major version (10.0.5 released 2026-07-22).
  Source: https://www.kicad.org/blog/2026/08/KiCad-Version-10.0.6-Release-Candidate-1-Available/

### Problems / risks identified
- Skipping the breadboard step is the most common way first boards fail. Prototype first.
- Draw footprints only from parts in hand, never from photos.
- Plan the mechanical side early: key spacing, knob clearance, USB at the edge, mounting holes.
- If the MCU module is mounted on the back of the board, its pinout is mirrored.
