### Found / learned
- Gateron's drawing is a bottom view (third-angle). Mirrored to the top view, its pad positions equal the siderakb and ai03 footprints exactly. The ai03 `MX_V2` hot-swap footprint is drawn for the back of the board, which is why it looked mirrored.
- "3-pin" on KS-33 listings means 2 metal terminals + the centre post (drawing circuit diagram shows 2 terminals).
- The ProdDeck file `Gateron-KS33-Solderable-Body.step` is Gateron's official model renamed (same size and CRC32 `c07b5433` as the archive on https://www.gateron.com/pages/3d). From it: housing base 0.30 mm above the PCB, top 8.90 mm, centre post 3.25 mm below the PCB top. The model's stem top differs from current Gateron photos (owner's screenshot): treat it as approximate.
- ai03 `MX_V2` solderable footprint rejected: NPTH Ø5.10 mm against a Ø5.05 ±0.05 mm post (drawing p. 1).
- EC11 reference vs KiCad stock footprint: pin pattern matches (2.5 mm pitch, 14.5 mm row spacing); pin hole (Ø1.2 vs 1.0 mm) and mounting tabs (about 10.4 vs 11.2 mm apart) differ. The real encoder decides.
- OLED listing (owner's paste): QWORK WD46221, 27.3 × 27.8 mm, active area 21.744 × 10.864 mm, about 25 mA on. No hole positions or header position given, so our own OLED footprint waits for calipers.
- RP2040 (Table 625, p. 616): output high ≥ 2.62 V at the drive setting (default 4 mA, p. 612); all GPIO + QSPI sourcing ≤ 50 mA. With 330 Ω, at most 10 mA per LED and 40 mA for four, whatever the LED.
- JLCPCB assembly for four LEDs was looked at (owner's question): setup fee $8 to about $25 plus feeder fees (https://jlcpcb.com/solutions/pcb-prototype-assembly, checked 2026-10-30). Not chosen.
- **Corrections to Claude's own statements earlier in this session:** (1) "footprint rotated 180° / the two ai03 footprints disagree" was wrong (view direction and KiCad's Y-down axis were ignored); (2) "the ProdDeck STEP has stray geometry and is 32 mm tall" was wrong (axis and control points were counted; vertices give 15 × 15 × 12.15 mm); (3) the guess that the third pin is an LED pad was wrong. All three were corrected in the session and in `parts.md`.
