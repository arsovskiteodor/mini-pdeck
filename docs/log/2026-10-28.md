### Found / learned
- **Diotronic Alps EC12E2430** has 0 detents and no push switch, and uses the EC12 footprint, not EC11.
  Sources: https://uk.farnell.com/alps/ec12e2430804/encoder-vertical-12mm-0det-24ppr/dp/2065070 ,
  https://befr.rs-online.com/web/p/mechanical-rotary-encoders/7295842
- **Gateron KS-33 Low Profile 2.0** uses its own footprint, compatible with neither MX nor Choc.
  The Amazon listing's "for MX" is wrong. Footprints: https://github.com/siderakb/key-switches.pretty
  (KS-27 and KS-33 share one). Source: https://keeb.io/products/gateron-ks-33-low-profile-2-0-switches
- **Waveshare 0.96" OLED (B)** (Diotronic MDIO9092) ships in 4-wire SPI mode. I2C needs a resistor
  moved on the back. Source: https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/0.96inch_OLED_(A)
- **Velleman VMA438** is a good I2C OLED (pin order VCC, GND, SCL, SDA per its manual) but costs €18.45.
  Source: https://cdn.velleman.eu/downloads/29/vma438_a4v01.pdf
- **"Low-profile EC11"**: no EC11 is truly low. The height comes from the shaft and knob. Options:
  a 15 mm shaft (e.g. Bourns PEC11L), cutting the shaft, a horizontal EC11B, or a roller encoder
  (Panasonic EVQWGD001, discontinued). Chose a standard EC11 and deal with knob height later (STATUS Q6).
- **USB behaviour:** under QMK the board enumerates as a USB HID device (keyboard, plus consumer
  control for media keys, optional mouse and Raw HID). No driver needed. Holding BOOT while plugging in
  gives the `RPI-RP2` drive for flashing UF2 files.
- **Remapping without reflashing:** QMK dynamic keymap plus VIA over Raw HID. See ADR-0008.

### Found / learned
- QMK MSYS runs in the **UCRT64** MSYS2 environment (`C:/QMK_MSYS/ucrt64.ini`). Running its tools from
  another MSYS2 environment fails with misleading errors (missing DLL).
- The cause of the first ARM failure is unknown. *Assumption:* a first-run problem such as antivirus
  scanning the newly unpacked toolchain. It did not come back.
- `waveshare/rp2040_keyboard_3` in the QMK repo is a 3-key RP2040 board with direct pins (`matrix_pins.direct`),
  the same structure as ADR-0006. It's a useful reference for our `keyboard.json`.
- QMK builds RP2040 boards against `RP_PICO_RP2040/board.c`, and compiles `stage2_bootloaders.c` (the flash-chip
  boot code). *Open:* whether the clone's flash chip needs a non-default stage-2 bootloader. Check this first if the
  clone flashes but doesn't boot.
