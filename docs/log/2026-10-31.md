### Found / learned
- **Default pad template:** `mini-pdeck.kicad_pro` → `defaults.pads` changed from 0.8 mm drill / 1.27 mm pad to
  5.25 / 5.25 mm. KiCad stores the last pad edited in Pad Properties as the template for pads added by hand on the
  board. It was the switch's Ø5.25 mm centre hole. No pad on the board changed (DRC: 0 footprint errors).
- **Switch centre hole:** it locates the fixed centre post (Ø5.05 ±0.05 mm, Gateron drawing p. 1, log 2026-10-30).
  The owner asked whether it is travel clearance: nothing we have read shows a moving part entering it.
- **Library links:** a `.kicad_pcb` stores a full copy of every footprint. The library link only matters for
  "update from library" and DRC's library-mismatch check.
- **Zone copy:** Ctrl+C/Ctrl+V pasted the B.Cu zone at the cursor, 19.87 mm right and 6.80 mm down. Its stale saved
  fill gave 38 clearance, 36 hole-clearance and 38 solder-mask-bridge errors (false shorts). Fixed with
  Zones → Duplicate Zone onto Layer, then refill (B) before saving.
- **LED placement:** D1–D4 sit within 0.025 mm of the centre of the switch footprint's LED window (`fp_rect` −3.2,
  −6.3 → 1.8, −4.05 mm, so the window centre is (−0.7, −5.175) mm from the switch centre). The four SW/LED courtyard
  overlaps are by design (ADR-0009); excluded in DRC.
- **Starved thermals** on ENC1/ENC2 pin C (GND), on F.Cu: first excluded, then gone after the stitching vias and the
  owner's changes. One F.Cu strip, from under U1 to ENC2 pin C, had been tied to ground by a single spoke.
- **Stitching:** nothing on this board is fast (USB runs on the module, `03-design.md` step 09), so no via spacing is
  required. The useful rule: each F.Cu piece tied to B.Cu at two or more points. About 10–15 mm between vias is a
  hobbyist rule of thumb (assumption, no source).
- The five DRC checks set to "ignore" (missing courtyard, track not centred on via, tuning profile, footprint
  filters, footprint type) are KiCad's defaults: the same set is in the project file of `dfef7d8`.
- **Corrections to Claude's statements in this session:** (1) briefing: "every new pad you place will start at that
  size" was overstated, as it applies only to pads added by hand on the board; (2) "a KiCad update could change the
  stock footprint without you noticing" was wrong, as the board keeps its own copy; (3) "182 track segments" was
  wrong, the count was 142; (4) ADR-0010 draft said H1 was near SW1, corrected to DISP1 before acceptance.
- Correction to [2026-10-30](2026-10-30.md), Problems: it says `lib/third_party/` was untracked. It was committed in
