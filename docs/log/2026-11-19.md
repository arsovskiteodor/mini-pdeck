### Found / learned
- QMK's volume and mute keycodes are consumer-page keys. They need `"extrakey": true`, or they compile and do
  nothing.
- `encoder_update_user()` is called once per `resolution` edges, so with resolution 4 the OLED counter counts
  detents. Counting one full turn (20) is a direct check of the resolution: 40 or 10 would mean 2 or 8.
- The encoder datasheet says hand soldering is "not recommended" (p. 1); both work after hand soldering (owner).

### Decisions

  with ENC1 so the top knob does volume. Owner to decide; a keymap change only.
