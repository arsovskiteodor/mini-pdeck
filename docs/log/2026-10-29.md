### Found / learned
- RP2040 internal pulls are 50 to 80 kΩ (Table 625, §5.5.3.4, p. 616). The datasheet says external pull-ups should be on the board, since the internal pulls may not be strong enough (§4.3, p. 441).
- NXP UM10204 Table 10 (p. 44 of 62): tr max 1000 ns Standard, 300 ns Fast, 120 ns Fast-mode Plus; Cb max 400 pF. §7.1 (p. 50 of 62): Rp(max) from T = 0.8473 × RC. Example (p. 52 of 62): Rp(min) = (VDD − VOL) / IOL.
- With VDD 3.3 V and VOL 0.4 V at 3 mA, Rp(min) ≈ 0.97 kΩ. Rp(max) is about 3.5 kΩ at 100 pF Cb and 1.8 kΩ at 200 pF (Fast-mode). Bus capacitance is not measured, so the external value stays TBD. The NXP equation images were not extracted, so check them by hand.
- The photo's 0x78 and 0x7A labels match the 8-bit write addresses for 0x3C and 0x3D, as the §8.1.5 text implies. Which pad is bridged on the received module is still unknown.
- The photo's board is marked "HW-239", but the DISP1 row says QWORK. Not confirmed as the same module.
