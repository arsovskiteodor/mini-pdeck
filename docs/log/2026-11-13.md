### Found / learned
- The PEC11L, EN11, ACZ11 and PEC11R share the EC11 pin pattern: A–C–B at 2.5 mm, switch pins 5 mm apart, 14.5 mm
  between rows (their datasheets). Their recommended tab holes differ: 12.8 / 11.1 / 9.4 / 13.2 mm. Ours are 11.2 mm.
- **PEC11L 3D model** (inch units, measured from its vertices): seating plane to shaft tip **19.6 mm**; pins 3.55 mm
  below the seating plane; A–C–B 7.5 mm and switch pins 7.0 mm from the shaft axis. Tab legs at 5.5–5.97 mm from
  the axis (11.0–11.9 mm apart), inside our slots (4.85–6.35 mm). Snap barbs below the board reach 6.92 mm, so they
  have to flex about 0.6 mm. Model, not measurement.
- **Correction to earlier in this session:** I first said the PEC11L tabs (12.8 mm) "don't match" the 11.2 mm slots and
  would need bending. The 3D model shows 12.8 mm is the spacing of the recommended holes, which are wider than the
  tabs. The tab legs themselves fit. Corrected in `parts.md`.
- Estimated shaft tops (drawing readings, ±1 mm): PEC11L 15 mm ≈ 19.5, EN11 15 mm ≈ 21.6, ACZ11 15 mm ≈ 21.5,
  PEC11R 15 mm ≈ 21.5. The old stand-in model (PEC11R, 20 mm) was 26.6 mm.
- PEC11L datasheet p. 1: hand soldering "not recommended"; contact bounce up to 10 ms at 60 RPM; use within 6 months
  of delivery. ACZ11 datasheet p. 1 allows hand soldering (350 °C, 3 s).
- The SamacSys licence forbids redistributing its models as library components, so the STEP and the zip stay out of
  git. The SamacSys KiCad footprint wasn't used: its tab holes are Ø2.52 mm at 11.4 mm, which matches neither the
  datasheet nor ours.
- Unikey Electronics Pte. Ltd. (Singapore) is listed as an *independent* distributor (self-published rankings; listed
  on Octopart); no independent reviews found.
- RS and Unikey pages block automated fetching; the RS page didn't finish loading in Chrome either. The owner
  supplied screenshots instead.
