### Found / learned
- The Pro Micro used on 2026-11-04/05 is the 5 V version (`parts.md`, DISP1), so it is not a valid source for the
  LED test: the board drives the LEDs from a 3.3 V RP2040 GPIO.
- Lighting from 3.3 V through 220 Ω shows Vf < 3.3 V at that current, so the listing's "3.5 V" looks wrong. Vf itself
  is **not measured** (Q12 stays open for the number). The 5 V + transistor fallback (ADR-0009 "Revisit if") now looks
  unlikely but is not ruled out until the GPIO test.
- A GPIO driven high gives less than the 3V3 pin (≥ 2.62 V at 4 mA drive, RP2040 datasheet RP-008371-DS-1, Table 625,
  p. 616, as cited in `parts.md`), so on the board the LED will be dimmer than in this test.
- 220 Ω worst case: (3.3 − 2.2) V / 220 Ω = 5 mA per LED, 20 mA for four, under the RP2040's 50 mA total.
- The working tree already held uncommitted `parts.md` changes dated 2026-11-08 (U1 arrived 2026-11-08; ruler
  measurement 23 × 18 mm; owner decision to check pin geometry by test-fitting into Rev A; switches expected
  2026-11-12). There is no 2026-11-08 log entry for them. Recorded here as found; STATUS.md now reflects them.
