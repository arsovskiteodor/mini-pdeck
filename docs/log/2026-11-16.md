### Found / learned
- **The new model is a simplified FreeCAD model, not Gateron's.** STEP header: FreeCAD, 2023-06-30, product
  `Gateron_KS_33_v3`. Parts: Base, Stem, Cap (the switch's top housing, not a keycap), PinA, PinB. Read from the
  STEP file's vertex coordinates and assembly placements, not measured:
  - Origin at the switch centre; housing bottom at Z = −2.5 mm, flat (no feet), 14 × 14 mm. Hence the +2.5 mm offset.
  - Centre post Ø5.05 mm (matches the drawing's 5.05 ±0.05 mm, `parts.md`), 3.2 mm below the PCB top.
  - Pins are flat blades, 1.00 × 0.45 mm. Pin 1 starts at the pad centre (0.225 mm off centre). Pin 2 is centred
    at Y = 5.375 mm. The drawing gives 5.75 mm and a round Ø1.00 mm terminal, so the model is 0.375 mm off there,
    and one corner touches the 1.5 mm hole wall in 3D. The pins' 2.0 mm-wide shoulders reach 0.5 mm into the board.
  - Stem top 9.55 mm above the PCB. The official model gives 8.90 mm.
  - LED window X −3.2 to 1.8 mm, matching the footprint's F.Fab outline; Y up to 6.5 mm (outline: 6.3 mm).
  - Conclusion: fine for renders, not a source of dimensions. Q6 heights come from calipers.
- **Update Footprint has side effects on the board.** Besides the model, the SW1–SW4 update:
  - removed `knockout` from the four reference labels on F.SilkS (visible on the next fab);
  - removed a `(units (unit (name "A") (pins "1" "2")))` block from each switch (schematic link data; whether
    "Update PCB from Schematic" restores it is unconfirmed);
  - reset SW2's hidden official-model offset from Z −2.75 mm to the library's −3.25 mm. The −2.75 mm had been
    there since `a35289d`, unlike the other three switches.
- **Keycap compatibility with the KS-33:** the KS-33 has an MX-style stem, but "not every MX keycap will clear
  the switch housing without collisions"; Keeb.io's LPF and THT families fit; MBK, MCC and DDC (Choc V1) don't
  (https://blog.keeb.io/low-profile-keycap-options-what-works-with-what/). Keychron's K Pro / K Max low-profile
  boards use Gateron Low Profile 2.0 (https://keychron.com/products/low-profile-gateron-mechanical-switch-set?page=2).
- The chosen keycaps are sold for full-height Gateron MX switches. KS-33 fit (skirt vs housing at full travel)
  is unknown until the one-key test on arrival. Bought under Amazon.es's 30-day return (owner).
- Owner's photo of a KS-33 stem top: a round red disc with an MX "+", matching the "pill" noted in `parts.md`
  (SW1–SW4, 3D model note).
